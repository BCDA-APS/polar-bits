id4_common.gui.diffract3d#

3D model of the POLAR diffractometer.

Ported from ~/jwkim/python/diffract/diffractometer_polar.py. The geometry constants and the kinematic chain are kept exactly as written there – they were matched against the real instrument, so they are not something to re-derive.

Lab frame (Bluesky convention):
+x

incident beam direction

+y

horizontal, perpendicular to the beam

+z

vertical up

Five angles, not six. The model’s joints are mu, chi, phi, delta, nu: there is no eta, and phi rotates about z where the 4S+2D model rotates it about y. The solver’s sixth real axis, tau, is not modelled – nothing in the drawing moves with it – and the solver’s gamma is this model’s nu. That last rename is why the panel maps names through id4_common.gui.config.MODEL_AXIS_MAP rather than matching them directly.

vtk and pyvista are imported lazily so this module – and therefore the GUI as a whole – still imports when they are not installed. AVAILABLE says whether the 3D view can actually be built.
